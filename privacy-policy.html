Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to all mobile apps published by SusaSoftX on Google Play and the Apple App Store (“the apps”).

Who is responsible

SusaSoftX is the name under which Max Söderström, an independent developer in Finland, publishes apps. Contact: susasoftx@gmail.com

Information I collect

I do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The apps do not require an account, and I do not run any servers that receive data from the apps. Content and settings in the apps stay on your device.

Advertising

Some apps show ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the device’s advertising ID
  • IP address and an approximate location based on it
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system and app version)
  • ad interactions (ads shown and tapped) and diagnostic data

This data is processed by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the apps ask for your consent before your data is used for personalised ads. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads, which rely on limited data for purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement.

You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads), where you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because I do not hold personal data about you, there is normally nothing for me to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google, you can use Google’s own privacy tools. You can always contact me with questions at susasoftx@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
